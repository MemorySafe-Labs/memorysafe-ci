Use two separate controls. Do not combine acceptance of the legal documents with consent to Automatic Mode.

## Control 1 - Beta agreement

**Checkbox label**

I agree to the MemorySafe Beta Terms of Use and acknowledge the MemorySafe Beta Privacy Policy.

**Supporting text**

MemorySafe Beta is experimental. Memory content is stored on this computer, in one database shared by the assistants you connect. Memory content reaches the assistant you are using, and whatever it includes in its own conversation goes to that assistant's provider under your agreement with them.

**Button**

Accept and continue

## Control 2 - Optional Automatic Mode

**Heading**

Turn on Automatic Mode?

**Consent text**

When Automatic Mode is on, MemorySafe may save short, directly stated, durable, non-sensitive facts from chats where MemorySafe is selected. It does not independently copy complete conversations or read an assistant's own built-in memory. Experimental filters attempt to avoid secrets, financial and government identifiers, contact details, precise addresses, and medical or health information. You can review saved memories and turn Automatic Mode off at any time. Turning it off stops future automatic captures but does not erase memories already stored.

**Primary button**

Turn on Automatic Mode

**Secondary button**

Not now

## Settings copy

**Automatic Mode on**

Automatic Mode is ON. MemorySafe may save eligible facts only in chats where MemorySafe is selected.

**Automatic Mode off**

Automatic Mode is OFF. New memories are saved only when you explicitly ask.

**Deletion note**

The current Forget action removes a memory from active recall. For complete deletion of all local beta data, stop the connector and securely delete the local MemorySafe database. Contact contact@memorysafe.ca for help.
